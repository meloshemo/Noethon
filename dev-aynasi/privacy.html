Privacy Policy — Dev Aynası (Hall of Giants)

Last updated: 28 August 2026

In short

Dev Aynası collects no personal data. There is no account, no email, no

advertising or analytics tracker. Everything the game remembers — your

progress, your best times, your settings — stays on your own device.

1. Data we handle

1.1 Single player

Nothing is sent to any server. The following is stored in your browser's local

storage, on your device only:

KeyContents
dev-aynasi:en-iyi:<room>Your best time for that room, in seconds
dev-aynasi:acilanThe highest room you have unlocked
dev-aynasi:devamAn unfinished run: room, hall seed, elapsed time
dev-aynasi:sonsuz-rekorYour record number of rooms in the endless hall
dev-aynasi:ayarlarBrightness, "reduced motion" and auto-resolution preferences
dev-aynasi:dilYour language choice (Turkish or English)
dev-aynasi:cevrimiciThe nickname, room code and server address you typed into the online race fields

We never receive these values and never share them. Clearing your browser's

site data — on Android, clearing the app's data — deletes all of them.

The nickname in dev-aynasi:cevrimici is kept only to pre-fill the field for

your next race; it is sent nowhere unless you enter the online mode.

1.2 Online race (multiplayer)

When the online mode is enabled, only what the game needs to run is sent to the

server, and it is kept in memory only while the match is running:

  • The nickname you type (it does not have to be your real name)
  • Your position inside the hall (x, y) and your current room
  • The hall code and your time

When the room empties, all of it is discarded. There is no database, no

persistent record, and IP addresses are not logged.

2. What we never collect

Name, email, phone, address; GPS location, device or advertising identifiers;

contacts, photos, microphone or camera; advertising cookies or trackers.

3. Third parties

There are none. While running, the game makes no request to any outside

site. Everything — fonts included — ships inside the game, and it works the

same offline. (Before 1.8.0 the fonts loaded from Google Fonts and that

request exposed your IP address to Google. That connection is now gone.)

On Steam, Valve's own data practices apply; on Google Play, Google's do. The

game itself sends these stores no additional data.

3.1 Android build

The Android package is the web version of the game, unchanged, running inside

a WebView. Its only permission is INTERNET, used solely for the online

race. Single-player works with no connection at all. No advertising ID is

requested, there is no analytics SDK, and no crash reports are sent. Android's

own backup is enabled, so your best times may be included in your device

backup — that backup lives in your Google account and never reaches us.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.

5. Your rights

Because we process no personal data, there is no record to access or erase. You

can clear what is stored on your device at any time through your browser's

"clear site data" option.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published in the game's

repository and the date above is updated.
